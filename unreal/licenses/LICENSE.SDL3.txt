SDL3 (SystemARM64/libSDL3.so.0)

libSDL3.so.0 in this package is not the OldUnreal SDL3 build. It is built
from bmdhacks' SDL3 fork with an SDL2 backend, which runs SDL3 programs on
the firmware's own SDL2:

  https://github.com/bmdhacks/SDL/tree/sdl2-backend
  commit 6057d79baf8321bf190479a699655f06cc2a962f

Altered source (zlib license, clause 2): three patches are applied on top
of that commit. They are in source/sdl3shim/ of
https://github.com/newmca/unreal-portmaster:

  sdl3-sdl2-backend-fixes.patch
      From PortMaster-New (ports/railroadrampage), commit
      3fcdb796b828bfac8d62a40b24842b3215c60ab4. PortMaster-New is MIT
      licensed, Copyright (c) 2021 christianhaitian.
  sdl3-sdl2-backend-unrealgold-gl.patch
  sdl3-sdl2-backend-unrealgold-winsize.patch
      Written for this port.
