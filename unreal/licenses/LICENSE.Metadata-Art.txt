The files cover.jpg and screenshot.jpg are authentic in-game captures of
Unreal Gold content, made for this PortMaster package on 2026-10-05. No
generated or AI-made imagery was used.

Both were rendered by the OldUnreal 227k_15 engine (the amd64 Linux build)
with this package's own game data, its OpenGLDrv renderer build, and its
config/UnrealLinux.ini and config/User.ini. The engine ran in a headless test
container (Xvfb with Mesa llvmpipe).

screenshot.jpg shows NyLeve's Falls (NyLeve.unr), with a Skaarj warrior
attacking the player near the Nali village. The scene was staged with the
game's own console commands: god, then ghost and walk to reach the village,
then summon UnrealI.SkaarjWarrior. It was rendered at 1280x960 with
HudScaler=2 and downsampled to the PortMaster 640x480 metadata format. It was
not otherwise altered.

cover.jpg is a frame of the original Unreal intro flyby (Unreal.unr, the Nali
castle). It was rendered at 1920x1440 with the intro's logo overlay switched
off through the console. The frame was then:

  - cropped to a square;
  - darkened towards the bottom;
  - given the title "UNREAL GOLD", set in DejaVu Serif Bold (DejaVu fonts,
    Bitstream Vera license);
  - scaled to 480x480;
  - centred on a black 640x480 canvas, like the Unreal Tournament '99 port's
    cover.jpg.
